# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Nix to be a welcoming, constructive, and technically serious open-source project.

Participation in this project means treating other people with respect, even during disagreement, review pressure, or design debate.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, but require technical clarity.
- Welcome feedback, questions, and improvement.
- Keep collaboration focused on helping the project move forward.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Deliberate hostility, intimidation, or trolling
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated bad-faith disruption of project discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Severe or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion from the project spaces.

## Reporting

For now, report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository contact channels once the public repository is live.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, issue discussions, pull requests, review conversations, and other official community interactions around Nix.
